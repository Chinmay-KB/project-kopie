# Privacy policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Kopie does not collect, store or share any personal data.

## What the extension does with your text

Everything happens inside your browser. Kopie has no server, makes no network requests and contains no analytics or tracking.

- **Text you type or paste** is turned into a QR code on your device and is gone when the popup closes.
- **Clipboard:** when the popup opens, Kopie may read your clipboard to fill in the text box for you. The text is used only to draw the QR code.
- **Current tab address:** read only when you click "Use this page's link".
- **Selected text or links:** read only when you choose "Send to phone with Kopie" from the right click menu. The text is held in the browser's session storage for a few seconds so the popup can show it, then removed. It is never written to disk or synced.

Kopie cannot read the content of the pages you visit.

## Permissions

| Permission | Why it is needed |
| --- | --- |
| `activeTab` | To read the address of the current tab when you ask for it |
| `contextMenus` | To add the "Send to phone with Kopie" menu item |
| `storage` | To pass the selected text from the menu to the popup |
